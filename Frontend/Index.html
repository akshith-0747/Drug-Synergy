HOW IT WORKS

Project methodology.

Three biological and chemical perspectives are used to represent drug combinations.

01
⌬

Chemical Structure

Represents the chemical properties of drugs using molecular structures and numerical fingerprints.

  • SMILES representations
  • RDKit molecular processing
  • Morgan fingerprints
  • 1024-bit representation
02
⌁

Drug Signatures (DS)

Represents how drugs perturb gene expression and affect biological pathways.

  • LINCS L1000 data
  • Drug perturbation signatures
  • Gene-expression patterns
  • Biological response features
03
◎

Drug Resistance Signatures (DRS)

Represents gene-expression patterns associated with sensitivity and resistance to drugs.

  • GDSC drug response
  • IC50-based sensitivity
  • Resistance-associated expression
  • Cell-line context

ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Synergia.

A machine-learning research platform for anticancer drug-combination synergy prediction.

Our objective

Synergia explores the prediction of synergistic effects between anticancer drug combinations in cancer cell lines. It brings together chemical structure information, drug signatures and drug-resistance signatures to study drug-combination responses.

Research disclaimer

Synergia is an academic research prototype. Its predictions are computational estimates and must not be used as medical advice or as a substitute for clinical testing.
