HOW IT WORKS

Project methodology.

Three biological and chemical perspectives are used to represent drug combinations.

01
⌬

Chemical Structure

Represents the chemical properties of drugs using molecular structures and numerical fingerprints.

  • SMILES representations
  • RDKit molecular processing
  • Morgan fingerprints
  • 1024-bit representation
02
⌁

Drug Signatures (DS)

Represents how drugs perturb gene expression and affect biological pathways.

  • LINCS L1000 data
  • Drug perturbation signatures
  • Gene-expression patterns
  • Biological response features
03
◎

Drug Resistance Signatures (DRS)

Represents gene-expression patterns associated with sensitivity and resistance to drugs.

  • GDSC drug response
  • GDSC monotherapy response features
  • IC50-based sensitivity
  • Quality-filtered dose-response curves
  • Resistance-associated expression
  • Cell-line context

FULL-DATA PREDICTION PROFILE

Expanded v1.5 feature coverage

The active dataset contains 426,956 rows and 172 cell lines across 19 columns. Production XGBoost bundles are retained, while automatic prediction uses the best eligible full-data model for each feature view according to the current pair-held-out comparison. The table shows each view's feature-complete training rows and cell-line coverage.

Feature viewTraining rowsTraining cell lines
Structure426,956172 / 172
Drug Signatures (DS)142,024165 / 172
Drug Resistance Signatures (DRS)93,840170 / 172

You can type a drug or cell-line name, or choose a suggestion. DS and DRS have fewer usable rows where matching features are unavailable. The prediction result flags a selected cell line that was not present in that view's training rows.

Automatic prediction selects the lowest-error eligible full-data model separately for Structure, DS, and DRS, then marks the best supported view using pair-held-out validation RMSE and cell-line training coverage. The three estimates remain separate predictions, not an ensemble; unavailable features and cell lines outside a view's training coverage are flagged.

All automatic choices are based on internal held-out-pair validation. Candidate status means the model has not replaced the separately retained production XGBoost bundle. Independent-study validation is still needed for broader performance claims.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Synergia.

A machine-learning research platform for anticancer drug-combination synergy prediction.

Our objective

Synergia explores the prediction of synergistic effects between anticancer drug combinations in cancer cell lines. It brings together chemical structure information, drug signatures and drug-resistance signatures to study drug-combination responses.

Research disclaimer

Synergia is an academic research prototype. Its predictions are computational estimates and must not be used as medical advice or as a substitute for clinical testing.
